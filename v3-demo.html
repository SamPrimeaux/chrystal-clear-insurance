General Liability

General liability insurance can help protect your business from certain claims involving bodily injury, property damage, and personal or advertising injury.

  • Bodily Injury: If someone is injured on your business premises or as a result of your business activities, this policy can help cover medical expenses and legal costs if you’re sued.
  • Property Damage: If your business causes damage to someone else’s property, coverage can help with repair or replacement costs.
  • Personal and Advertising Injury: This includes certain claims related to defamation, libel, slander, or copyright infringement in advertising.

General liability insurance usually does not cover employee injuries, professional mistakes, or intentional acts of harm.

Professional Liability

Professional liability insurance provides coverage for certain professional errors, omissions, or negligence claims.

  • Can help cover legal fees, settlements, or judgments if a client claims a mistake caused financial harm.
  • May protect against claims of negligence in services provided.
  • Often tailored to specific professions, including healthcare providers, lawyers, accountants, consultants, and architects.

It does not cover physical injuries or property damage, and it does not cover intentional wrongdoing or fraudulent actions.

Commercial Auto Insurance

Commercial auto insurance helps protect vehicles used for business purposes.

  • Liability Coverage: Helps pay for legal costs, medical expenses, and repair bills if a business vehicle causes damage or injuries.
  • Collision Coverage: Helps pay for repairs to a business vehicle after an accident.
  • Comprehensive Coverage: Helps protect against theft, vandalism, natural disasters, and other non-collision incidents.
  • Hired/Non-Owned Auto Coverage: May help when employees use personal vehicles for business or when vehicles are rented for work.
Commercial Property

Commercial property insurance helps protect business property from covered causes of loss.

  • Buildings and business premises
  • Furniture, equipment, inventory, and business assets
  • Machinery, computers, and specialized tools
  • Outdoor assets such as signs, fences, or landscaping when included
  • Business interruption coverage after certain covered property damage

Floods, earthquakes, wear and tear, and maintenance issues usually require separate policies or endorsements.

Commercial Bonds

Commercial bonds, also known as business or commercial surety bonds, help ensure businesses comply with laws, regulations, or contractual obligations.

  • License and permit bonds
  • Fidelity bonds
  • Contract bonds, including bid, performance, and payment bonds
  • Public official bonds
  • Court bonds
  • Miscellaneous industry bonds
