# Chrystal Clear Insurance
**Built by [Inner Animal Media](https://inneranimalmedia.com)**

---

## Project Purpose

This website is designed to help Chrystal Clear Insurance look more professional, build trust quickly, and convert more visitors into real inquiries.

The goal is not just a better-looking website — it is a stronger business tool that helps visitors understand who Chrystal Clear Insurance serves, what solutions are offered, and how to take the next step.

A quality insurance website should answer three questions fast:

1. Can I trust this business?
2. Do they offer what I need?
3. How do I contact them or request a quote?

This project is built around those three outcomes.

---

## Core Goals

### 1. Build Immediate Trust
Insurance is personal. Visitors want to know they are working with someone reliable, professional, and easy to reach. The site should communicate professionalism, local credibility, clear services, and simple next steps — polished and modern without feeling cold or corporate.

### 2. Make Quote Requests Easier
Every major page guides the visitor toward one action: request a quote, call the business, send a message, or learn about available options. No guessing what to do next.

### 3. Prioritize Mobile
A large portion of visitors will arrive via phone — through Facebook, Google, referrals, or direct links. Mobile is treated as a first-class experience: clear buttons, readable text, fast-loading pages, simple navigation, click-to-call, and easy-to-find quote options.

### 4. Clarify the Services
Each service is presented in plain language so visitors quickly understand what is available and why they should reach out. No insurance jargon overload.

## Site Structure

### Page 1 — Home
The homepage is the main conversion page.

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hero | Business name, headline, supporting statement, primary CTA ("Request a Quote"), secondary CTA ("Call Now" / "View Services") |
| Trust | Why people choose Chrystal Clear Insurance — personal guidance, multiple options, local service, clear communication |
| Services Preview | Clean grid of main insurance categories with short descriptions and CTAs |
| About Preview | Short human-centered intro to the business |
| Closing CTA | "Not sure what coverage you need? Let's make it simple." |

### Page 2 — Services
Organized breakdown of all coverage options offered. Each section covers who it helps, what it protects, why it matters, and a quote CTA. Potential categories:

- Auto Insurance
- Home Insurance
- Life Insurance
- Business Insurance
- Health / Supplemental Insurance
- Additional coverage options

### Page 3 — About
Builds trust and connection. Covers who Chrystal Clear Insurance is, who they help, why the business exists, what clients can expect, and a friendly CTA. Not a corporate bio — a real introduction.

### Page 4 — Contact / Request a Quote
Makes it easy to take action.

**Features:** contact form, click-to-call, email, business hours, service area, social links

**Form fields:**
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Type of insurance needed
- Preferred contact method
- Short message
